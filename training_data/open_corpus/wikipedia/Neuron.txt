Neuron

A neuron, neurone, or nerve cell, is an electrically excitable cell that communicates with other cells by firing electric signals called action potentials. Neurons form neural networks in the nervous system, predominantly within the brain and spinal cord of the central nervous system. Neurons receive and conduct impulses, communicating with other cells via synapses. These are specialized connections that commonly use minute amounts of chemical neurotransmitters to pass the electric signal from the presynaptic neuron to the target cell through the synaptic gap.
