Periodic table

The periodic table, also known as the periodic table of the elements, is an ordered arrangement of the chemical elements into rows ("periods") and columns ("groups"). Elements in the same group tend to show similar chemical characteristics. The table is also divided into four roughly rectangular areas called blocks. The periodic table is an icon of chemistry, although it is also widely used in physics and other sciences. It is a depiction of the periodic law, which states that when the elements are arranged in order of their atomic numbers an approximate recurrence of their properties is evident.
